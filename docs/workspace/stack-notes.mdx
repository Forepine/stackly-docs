Stack Notes are sticky notes inside Stackly. Use them for campaign plans, meeting notes or a to-do list your team can share.

<Shot id="workspace/notes-list" title="Stack notes page with coloured note cards and the status tabs" />

## When to use it
- A bakery writes its festive-week plan: which links to share, on which day.
- An agency keeps a client checklist: logo received, domain set up, report sent.

## Create a note
1. In the left menu, under **Workspace**, click **Stack Notes**.
2. Click **New note**.
3. Type a **Title** (3 to 50 characters) and the **Content**. Both are required.
4. Pick a **Note colour**. It shows on the card in your list.
5. Under **Priority**, choose **Normal**, **Moderate**, **Priority** or **Completed**.
6. Add **Tags** if you like.
7. Under **Tasks**, click **Add task** for each to-do. Tick a task when it's done.
8. Under **Collaborators**, pick team members to share the note with.
9. Click **Create note**.

<Shot id="workspace/notes-form" title="New note form with colour, priority, tasks and collaborators" />

:::tip
Click **Templates** at the top of the form to start from a ready-made note, such as **Meeting Minutes & Action Items** or **Weekly Sprint & Standup**. You can change anything after.
:::

## Colours

- Note colours: **Default**, **Amber**, **Emerald**, **Blue**, **Purple**, **Rose**, **Orange** and **Slate**, or any custom colour.
- Each task can have its own colour too.

## Find a note
- Use the tabs **All**, **Normal**, **Moderate**, **Priority** and **Completed** to filter by priority.
- Choose **My Items** to see only notes you created.
- Click the pin (**Pin to top**) to keep a note at the top.

## Edit a note
1. Click the pencil (**Edit note**) on the note.
2. Make your changes.
3. Click **Save changes**.

Click **Copy note** to copy the title, text and tasks, ready to paste into WhatsApp or email.

## Delete a note
1. Click the bin (**Delete note**).
2. Confirm.

:::caution
A deleted note can't be brought back.
:::

## Good to know
- How many notes you can keep depends on your plan. Deleting a note frees up space for a new one.
- Each note needs its own title. Two notes can't have the same title.
- Stack Notes have no reminders or due dates.
- If you've connected Slack or Discord in [Integrations](./integrations), your team gets a message when someone creates a note.
