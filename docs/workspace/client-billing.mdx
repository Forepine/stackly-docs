On the Agency plan you can bill every client from Stackly. Send an invoice, or set up a monthly retainer so it goes out by itself. The client pays you directly by UPI or bank transfer, and you mark it paid. Stackly never holds the money.

## Set up billing <PlanBadge plan="Agency" />
1. In the left menu, under **Workspace**, click **Client Billing**.
2. Open the **Settings** tab.
3. Under **Your business on invoices**, add your business name, billing email, phone and address. Add your **GSTIN** if you charge GST, and check the **GST rate**.
4. Under **How clients pay you**, add your **UPI ID** and, if you like, your bank account and IFSC.
5. Click **Save settings**.

Invoices are numbered with your **Invoice number prefix**, for example `ACME-0001`, `ACME-0002`.

:::note
GST is added on top of the items as one line. Check with your accountant whether you need CGST and SGST shown separately, and what else your invoices must include.
:::

## Send an invoice
1. Click **New invoice**.
2. Pick the **Client**. Their email fills in from the client's details under **Clients**.
3. Set the **Invoice date** and **Due date**, and add the items. Switch on **Add GST** if it applies.
4. Click **Save & send** to email it now, or **Save draft** to finish later.

The client gets an email with your logo and a **View invoice & pay** button. Replies come to you.

You can also share an invoice yourself: open the **⋯** menu and click **Copy invoice link**, then paste it on WhatsApp.

## What the client sees
A page with the invoice, your details and how to pay:
- a **UPI QR code** for ₹ invoices, and a **Pay with a UPI app** button on phones
- your **bank details**, each with a copy button
- **Download PDF**, which saves the invoice as a PDF

## Mark it paid
When the money arrives, click **Paid** on the invoice (or **⋯ → Mark paid**). Add the date and, if you like, the UTR. If you marked one by mistake, use **⋯ → Mark unpaid**.

## Monthly retainers
1. Open the **Retainers** tab and click **New retainer**.
2. Pick the client, the items, the day of the month (1 to 28) and how many days they have to pay.
3. Click **Save retainer**.

On that day every month, at 9 AM IST, an invoice is made and emailed to the client. If the client has no email saved, the invoice stays as a draft and the retainer shows a note.

## Reminders and paused reports
- If an invoice isn't paid, the client gets a reminder 1, 3 and 7 days after the due date.
- In **Settings → Pause reports when an invoice is overdue**, switch on the clients this applies to and choose after how many days. For those clients, shared reports and report emails pause while an invoice is that late. They come back the moment you mark it paid.
- Links and QR codes never stop working: they're for your client's customers.

## Good to know
- Client billing is on the **Agency** plan, for owners and admins.
- Up to 50 invoices a month are included. Contact us if you need more.
- Drafts and unpaid invoices can be edited (**⋯ → Edit**). A sent invoice keeps its number and link, so the client's page shows the change straight away; click **Save & send again** to email them too. Paid invoices can't be edited.
- Your UPI ID and bank details come from **Settings**, not the invoice: fix them there and every unpaid invoice shows the new details.
- Invoices show "Invoice sent with Stackly" at the bottom.
