The free Stackly extension for Chrome makes a short link for the page you're looking at, without opening Stackly. It's handy when you want to share a product page on WhatsApp quickly.

<Shot id="workspace/extension-popup" title="Extension popup after shortening, with the short link, Copy, Open and QR buttons" />

## Install it
1. Open the [Stackly extension on the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/stackly-url-shortener/enioiildcgagplnkphneffclebkemcob). You can also click **Get the extension** in the Stackly left menu.
2. Click **Add to Chrome**, then **Add extension**.
3. Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin **Stackly - URL Shortener** so it's always in view.

## Sign in
1. Click the Stackly icon in the toolbar.
2. Click **Login**.
3. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**, then click **Sign In**.

When you're signed in, your links are saved to your Stackly account, so you can see their clicks.

## Shorten the current tab
1. Go to the page you want to share.
2. Click the Stackly icon. The page address shows under **Current URL**.
3. If you're signed in, you can type a **Link Title** (optional).
4. Click **Shorten URL**.

Two quicker ways:
- Press <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd>.
- Right-click the page, or any link on it, and choose **Shorten with Stackly**.

A tick on the Stackly icon means it worked. Open the extension to see the link.

## Copy the link or show a QR code
1. After shortening, click **Copy** to copy the short link.
2. Click **QR** to show a QR code that people can scan. Click **Hide QR** to close it.
3. Click **Open** to test the link in a new tab, or **Shorten another URL** to start again.

Your latest links are listed under **Recent Links**. Click **Clear** to empty the list.

## Good to know
- The extension is free and works without signing in. Links made without signing in stop working after 24 hours.
- It can't shorten Chrome's own pages, such as settings or the new tab page.
- **Recent Links** is kept only in this browser.

## Troubleshooting
- **"This page can't be shortened by Stackly."** → You're on a Chrome page or a file on your computer. Open a normal website.
- **A red mark on the Stackly icon** → The shortcut or right-click didn't work. Open the extension and click **Shorten URL** instead.
