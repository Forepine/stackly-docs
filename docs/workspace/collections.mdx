A collection is a folder for your links and vCards. Make one per client, campaign or project so you can find everything fast.

<Shot id="workspace/collections-list" title="Collections page in card view with a few collections" />

## When to use it
- An agency keeps one collection per client: "Sharma Sweets", "Pune Yoga Studio".
- A shop groups all its Diwali sale links in a "Diwali campaign" collection.

## Create a collection
1. In the left menu, under **Workspace**, click **Collections**.
2. Click **New collection** (or **Create Your First Collection** if you have none yet).
3. Type a **Name**, for example "Diwali campaign".
4. Add a **Description** and **Tags** if you like. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or a comma after each tag.
5. Click **Create collection**.

## Add a link to a collection
1. Go to your links (or vCards).
2. Open the **⋯** menu on the link.
3. Click **Move to collection**.
4. Under **Select Destination Collection**, pick the collection.
5. Click **Move to Collection →**.

<Shot id="workspace/collections-move" title="The Move to Collection window with a collection picked" />

A link can be in one collection at a time. To move it to another one, first click **Remove from collection** in its **⋯** menu, then move it again.

## Open a collection
1. Click the collection's card or row.
2. Use the tabs **All**, **Links** and **vCards** to filter what's inside.
3. Each item shows its **Clicks**, when it was **Updated** and when it **Expires**.

<Shot id="workspace/collections-open" title="Inside a collection, showing the All, Links and vCards tabs and the list of items" />

## Remove a link from a collection
1. Inside the collection, click **Remove from collection** on the item.
2. Confirm. The link itself isn't deleted.

## Rename or edit a collection
1. Click the pencil (**Edit collection**) on the collection.
2. Change the name, description or tags.
3. Click **Save changes**.

## Delete a collection
1. Click the bin (**Delete collection**).
2. Confirm.

The links and vCards inside are kept. They just leave the collection, and you still find them on your Links and vCards pages.

## Good to know
- Names need 2 to 50 characters, and each name can be used only once.
- Descriptions can be up to 500 characters.
- Collections are free on every plan.
