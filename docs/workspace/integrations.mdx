Send link updates to the apps your team already uses. For example, a shop can post a Slack message each time someone makes a new link, or keep every link and click in a Google Sheet.

<Shot id="workspace/integrations-page" title="Integrations page with the Discord, Slack, Google Sheets and Chrome Extension cards" />

## What you can connect

| Integration | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Discord** | Posts alerts to a channel in your Discord server. |
| **Slack** | Posts alerts to a channel in your Slack workspace. |
| **Google Sheets** | Adds a row to a sheet for each new link. It can also add clicks and visiting card leads. |
| **Chrome Extension** | Shortens the page you're on. See [Browser extension](./browser-extension). |

Cards marked **Coming Soon** (Custom Webhooks, Zapier, Make and Notion & Airtable) aren't ready yet.

## Connect Slack or Discord <PlanBadge plan="Growth" />
1. In the left menu, under **Workspace**, click **Integrations**.
2. On the card, click **Connect Slack** or **Connect Discord**.
3. Click **Proceed to Authorize** and allow Stackly in the window that opens.
4. For Discord, choose your server and click **Select Server**.
5. **Step 1 of 3**: pick the channel for alerts.
6. **Step 2 of 3**: choose which alerts to send. For **Short Links**, **vCards**, **Notes**, **Screenshots** and **SEO Updates**, turn **Create**, **Update** or **Delete** on or off. Then click **Save & Continue to Test**.
7. **Step 3 of 3**: click **Send Test Message** to check it works.

To change the channel later, click **Configure Channels**. For Discord, **Change Server** lets you pick another server.

## Connect Google Sheets <PlanBadge plan="Growth" />
1. On the Google Sheets card, click **Connect Google Sheets**.
2. Sign in with your Google account and allow access.
3. Click **Create New Sheet**, pick a sheet **From Drive**, or paste a sheet link under **Custom URL**.
4. Turn on what to sync: **Auto-Sync Newly Created Links**, **Auto-Sync Real-Time Link Clicks** or **Auto-Sync Digital vCard Leads**.
5. Click **Save Settings**.
   <Shot id="workspace/integrations-google-sheets" title="Google Sheets settings with the three sync switches" />

Click **Send Test Row** to check the sheet, or **Sync All Links** to add the links you already have.

## Disconnect an integration
1. On the integration's card, click **Disconnect**.
2. Confirm if asked. Alerts or syncing stop straight away.

## Good to know
- Integrations are on Growth and higher plans. On Free and Starter, the page asks you to upgrade.
- Only the account owner can connect, change or disconnect integrations. Other members see **(Owner Only)** on the button.
- You can send up to 5 test messages an hour.
