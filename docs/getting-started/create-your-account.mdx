A Stackly account is free to start, and you don't need a card. New accounts get a 7-day free trial (see [plans, limits and your free trial](./plans-and-limits)).

<Shot id="getting-started/signup" title="The Sign Up form with the Google and GitHub buttons" />

## Sign up with your email
1. Go to the Stackly sign-up page and make sure the **Sign Up** tab is selected.
2. Type your **Full Name** and **Work Email**.
3. Choose a **Password**: at least 8 characters, with 1 capital letter (A–Z) and 1 number (0–9), and no spaces. The bar under the box turns green when all three are met.
4. Click **Get Started for Free**.
5. You go to the **Sign In** page. Check your inbox for the Stackly welcome email and click **Activate Your Account**.

## Sign up with Google or GitHub
1. On the sign-up or sign-in page, under **or continue with**, click **Google** or **GitHub**.
2. Pick your account in the window that opens.

You're signed in straight away. Your email is already verified, so you can skip the next step.

## Verify your email
Until your email is verified, most buttons and the left menu are locked. A strip at the top of your dashboard says **Verify your email to unlock every feature**.

1. Click **Send verification email** in that strip.
2. Open the email from Stackly and click **Activate Account**.
3. Come back to Stackly and refresh the page.

<Shot id="getting-started/verify-banner" title="The verify-your-email strip at the top of the dashboard" />

No email? Check your spam folder, then click **Resend email**.

## Sign in
1. Open the **Sign In** tab.
2. Type your **Email address** and **Password**.
3. Tick **Keep me signed in** if this is your own device. You stay signed in for 7 days instead of 1.
4. Click **Sign In**.

## Reset a forgotten password
1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Type your **Email address** and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. Open the email and click the link. It works for 15 minutes.
4. Type a new **Password** and **Confirm Password**, then click **Update Password**.
5. Sign in with your new password.

<Shot id="getting-started/reset-password" title="The Reset password form" />

## Good to know
- Email addresses with a "+" in them (like `name+shop@gmail.com`) can't be used to sign up.
- Joining a teammate's workspace? Use the invite link in your email instead of signing up. See [team members](../account/team-members).
- Made a short link on the Stackly homepage before you had an account? Sign up from its **Keep it** prompt and the link is saved to your new account.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Already exist. Please login."** → You already have an account with this email. Sign in, or reset your password.
- **"It looks like you registered with Google or GitHub"** → Sign in with the same button you used to sign up. To add a password too, see [password and security](../account/password-and-security).
- **The reset link doesn't work** → Links last 15 minutes. Ask for a new one.
- **"Account is not verified"** → Send the verification email again from the dashboard and click the link inside.
