Stackly has five plans: **Free**, **Starter**, **Growth**, **Pro** and **Business**. Each plan sets how much you can create. For prices and exact numbers, see the [Pricing page](https://stackly.in/pricing), or **Billing & Plans** → **Upgrade plan** inside the app.

## What the limits mean
| Limit | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Links** | Short links you create. Monthly limits reset on your renewal date. |
| **Clicks** | Visits to your links in each billing cycle. You can buy more with a top-up. |
| **Visiting cards** | Digital visiting cards (e-cards). |
| **Event pages** | Event pages you've made. Free includes 2 in total. |
| **Custom domains** | Your own domains for short links. Not on Free or Starter. |
| **Team members** | People in your workspace, including you. |
| **Notes** | Stack Notes. |
| **Data retention** | How many days of analytics stay in your dashboard. Older data moves to an archive. See [data retention](../account/data-retention). |

**Business** has custom limits. Click **Talk to sales** on the plans page.

## Features that need a plan
- **UTM Builder:** Starter and above.
- **Campaigns**, **Leads**, **Brand Deals** and hiding Stackly branding: Growth and above. The menu shows a **Pro** tag on these until you upgrade.
- **Integrations** (Google Sheets, Slack, Discord) and downloading archived data: Growth and above.
- **API keys:** not on Free or Starter.

## Your free trial
Every new account starts on **Free** with a 7-day trial. The plan chip at the top of the screen shows **Trial Active** and the days left.

When the 7 days end and you're still on Free:
- You can't create new short links, bulk links, event pages or personal links.
- Everything you already made keeps working: links still open, event pages stay online and personal links still work.
- The chip changes to **Trial Expired**. Click it to upgrade.

Upgrade to any paid plan to start creating again. See [billing and plans](../account/billing-and-plans).

## Event pages on Free
Free includes **2 event pages in total**. Deleting one doesn't give the slot back. For example, a yoga studio that makes 2 events and deletes one still can't make a third without upgrading. On paid plans, deleting an event frees its slot.

## See your usage
- **Dashboard**: the **Plan usage** box shows this month. Use the arrows to see earlier months.
- **Billing & Plans**: the **Usage** section shows each limit with how much is left.

## Good to know
- If a paid plan ends without renewal, the chip shows your plan name with **Expired**, and you can't create new items until you renew.
- At 100% of a limit you'll see **Limit reached**. Upgrade, or for clicks, buy a top-up.
