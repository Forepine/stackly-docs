After you sign in, you land on **Dashboard**: your home page with shortcuts, plan usage and recent activity. The menu on the left takes you to everything else.

<Shot id="getting-started/dashboard-home" title="The home dashboard with greeting, plan usage and activity" />

## The home dashboard
- **Greeting and shortcuts:** **New link**, **New e-card** and **New note** open the create form straight away.
- **Plan usage:** what you've used this month for links, custom domains, e-cards, notes and team seats. Use the arrows to see earlier months, and **This month** to come back.
- **Creation activity:** a calendar of the days you created things, with your **Current streak** and **Longest streak**. Filter by **Links**, **Cards**, **Notes**, **Domains** or **Team**.
- **Your plan:** your plan name and trial or renewal status, with **Upgrade to Pro** or **Manage subscription**.
- **Go further:** quick links to **Analytics**, **Integrations** and the free **Chrome extension**.

## The account menu
Click your name at the bottom of the left menu.

<Shot id="getting-started/account-menu" title="The account menu open at the bottom of the left menu" />

- **Preferences**: your profile and settings. See [your profile](../account/profile).
- **Billing & Plans**: see [billing and plans](../account/billing-and-plans).
- **Team Members**: see [team members](../account/team-members).
- **Help & Docs**: opens this help center.
- **Copy Email**: copies your sign-in email.
- **Log Out**: signs you out.

Your photo at the top right opens a similar menu with **Settings**, **Billing**, **Team**, **Analytics** and **Log out**.

## The top bar
- **Search:** filters the list on the page you're on, such as your links or notes. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) to jump to it.
- **Plan chip:** shows **Trial Active** with the days left, **Free Plan**, your plan name with days left, or **Trial Expired**. Click it to open billing.
- **Bell:** live click alerts (link, country and device) while Stackly is open. Click **Clear all** to empty the list.
- **Sun / moon:** switches between light and dark mode.

## Use it on a phone
1. Tap the menu icon at the top left to open the left menu.
2. Tap the magnifier icon to search.
3. Tap your photo at the top right for the account menu.

<Shot id="getting-started/dashboard-phone" title="The dashboard on a phone with the left menu open" device="phone" />

## Good to know
- The plan chip shortens to "5d left" on small screens.
- Click alerts aren't saved. They clear when you close or refresh the page.
