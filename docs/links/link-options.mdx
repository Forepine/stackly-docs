Under **More options** in the link form you can add notes, lock a link, limit who can open it, and tag its traffic. Click a group to open it. Its header shows what's set.

<Shot id="links/more-options" title="More options with Notes, Security, Targeting and UTM tracking" />

## The options
| Option | Where | When to use it | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notes | **Notes** | A reminder for you and your team, like "For the Pune flyer". Visitors never see it. Up to 200 characters. | All plans |
| Password | **Security** → **Password** | Share something private, like a price list for wholesale buyers. Visitors type the password first. | Starter+ |
| Expiry date | **Security** → **Expires on** | An offer that ends, like a weekend sale. After that date and time, visitors see an expired page. | Starter+ |
| Operating system | **Targeting** → **Operating system** | An app download link only for Android or iOS. Choose from Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android. | Starter+ |
| Browser | **Targeting** → **Browser** | A page that only works well in some browsers, like Chrome or Safari. | Starter+ |
| Country | **Targeting** → **Country** | An offer that's only valid in India. | Starter+ |
| UTM tags | **UTM tracking** | See in Google Analytics which post or ad sent the visitor. | Starter+ |
| Open in app | Below **Destination URL** | Links to YouTube, Instagram and similar open in the app on phones. | All plans |

On the Free plan, locked groups show a **Starter** label and an **Upgrade to Starter** button.

## Add a password or expiry date <PlanBadge plan="Starter" />
1. Open **More options** → **Security**.
2. In **Password**, type a password of at least 6 characters, with no spaces. Click the eye icon to check it.
3. In **Expires on**, pick a date and time.
4. Click **Create link** (or **Save changes** when editing).

## Limit who can open the link <PlanBadge plan="Starter" />
1. Open **Targeting**.
2. Pick one or more values in **Operating system**, **Browser** or **Country**. Type to search the list.
3. Save the link.

A visitor must match **every** rule you set. Everyone else sees a page explaining why. See [what visitors see](./what-visitors-see).

## Add UTM tags <PlanBadge plan="Starter" />
1. Fill in **Destination URL** first.
2. Open **UTM tracking** and click **Choose a template or add UTM tags**.
3. Pick a saved template in **Load Saved Preset Template**, or fill in **Source**, **Medium** and **Campaign** (required), plus **Term**, **Content** and **Referral** if you need them.
4. Click **Apply Parameters**.

To save templates for reuse, see [UTM Builder](../grow/utm-builder).

## Open links in the app
When you paste a link to X (Twitter), YouTube, Instagram, Spotify, Amazon, Telegram or LinkedIn, an **Open in the … app** card appears. On a phone, visitors see an **Opening …** page that takes them into the app instead of a browser inside WhatsApp or Instagram. They can tap **Continue in browser** instead.

## Good to know
- **Click limits** aren't set in this form. Your plan gives every link a click limit. See [manage your links](./manage-links#click-limits-and-top-ups).
- If you leave **Expires on** empty, the link is still saved with an end date 15 days after you create it. Check the date on the link and edit it if you need longer.
- To get alerts when someone clicks, use the bell icon on the link in your list.
