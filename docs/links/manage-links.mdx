The **Links** page lists every short link with its clicks and status. Each link has a row of quick actions on the right.

<Shot id="links/list-view" title="Links page in list layout with filters and actions" />

## Find a link
- **List or grid:** click the list or grid icon next to **New link**. Stackly remembers your choice.
- **Search:** type in **Search** at the top of the page (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>, or <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac). It matches the back-half, title and destination.
- **Status filters:** **All**, **Active**, **Paused**, **Pinned**, **Expired**, and **Near limit** (only shown when a link is close to its click limit). Each shows a count.
- **Everyone's / Mine:** account owners can switch between all the team's links and only their own.

Filters only check links already loaded. Scroll to load more, or click **Clear filters**.

## Work with a link
| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Copy | Click the copy icon next to the short link. |
| Open | Click the open icon, or **⋯** → **Open short link**. |
| Pause or resume | Flip the switch in the **Status** column. Paused links show **Paused** and stop opening. |
| Pin to the top | Click the pin icon. Find pinned links with the **Pinned** filter. |
| Click alerts | Click the bell icon to turn alerts on or off. |
| Edit | Click the pencil icon, change things, then click **Save changes**. |
| QR code | Click the QR icon. See [QR codes](./qr-codes). |
| Send by email | **⋯** → **Send by email**. |
| Share stats with a client | **⋯** → **Share click report**. See [client report link](../insights/client-report-link). |
| Collections | **⋯** → **Move to collection** or **Remove from collection**. |

Copy, open, pin, alerts and QR only work while the link is active.

## Change the back-half
1. Click the pencil icon on the link.
2. Change the **Short link** field (and anything else you like).
3. Click **Save changes**.

:::caution
The old link and any printed QR code stop working straight away.
:::

## Delete a link
1. Click **⋯** → **Delete**.
2. Click **Delete** to confirm.

:::caution
Deleting can't be undone.
:::

## See clicks
The **Clicks** column shows total clicks, and the click limit if the link has one (for example **820 / 1,000**). For charts by country, device and source, open **Analytics** or **Reports** in the left menu. See [analytics](../insights/analytics).

## Click limits and top-ups
Your plan gives each link a click limit. When a link has less than 10% of its clicks left:
- A **"… is going viral"** banner appears at the top, and the link shows **Going viral · N clicks left**.
- At the limit, the link shows **Limit reached, redirects stopped** and visitors see a "limit reached" page.

To add clicks:
1. Click **Top up this link** in the banner, **Top up** on the link, or **⋯** → **Add more clicks**.
2. In **Boost your clicks**, choose a booster and the links (or vCards) that get the clicks.
3. Check your name, email and mobile, then click **Request top-up**.

No payment now: our team emails an invoice, then switches on the clicks.

## If your free trial has ended
You'll see **Your free trial has ended** at the top, and **New link** is greyed out. Your existing links keep working and you can still manage them. Click **Upgrade plan** to create new links.

## Good to know
- You can't change a link's destination. Create a new link instead.
- Pausing keeps the link and its stats. Flip the switch back on to resume.
