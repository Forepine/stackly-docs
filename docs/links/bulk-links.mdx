Have a list of product pages or blog posts? The **Bulk** tab makes up to 25 short links at once. Example: a clothing shop shortening every item in its new collection for an Instagram story series.

<Shot id="links/bulk-tab" title="Bulk tab with several URLs added to the list" />

## Create links in bulk <PlanBadge plan="Starter" />
1. In the left menu, click **Links**.
2. Click **New link**.
3. At the top right of the form, click **Bulk**.
4. In **Destination URLs**, paste your links, one per line or separated by commas.
5. Click **Add to list**. Your links appear below as **N URLs ready**. Click the bin icon to remove one.
6. Optional: pick a **Domain**.
7. Type a **Title**. Each link gets a number added: "Summer drop-1", "Summer drop-2" and so on.
8. Optional: add **Tags** and open **More options** for notes, security, targeting or UTM tags. They apply to every link in the batch.
9. Click **Create N links**.

You're taken back to the **Links** page, where the new links appear.

<Shot id="links/bulk-created" title="Links page showing a batch of numbered bulk links" />

## Good to know
- You need **2 to 25** links per batch. Each one must start with `https://` or `http://`.
- Duplicates and broken addresses are skipped, with a warning.
- You can't choose the back-halves in bulk. Stackly makes them for you, and they're longer than normal ones. If you want a nice back-half like `/summer-sale`, create that link on the **Single** tab, or edit it afterwards.
- The same UTM tags are added to every link in the batch.
- Each link counts toward your plan's link total. If you don't have enough left for the whole batch, nothing is created and you'll see a message.
- On the Free plan, the **Bulk** tab shows a lock and **Upgrade to Starter**.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Add at least 2 URLs to create links in bulk"** → Add another link, or use the **Single** tab for one.
- **"You don't have enough limit left…"** → Remove some links from the list, or upgrade your plan.
- **An error appears when I click Create** → Make sure you clicked **Add to list** after pasting, and that **Title** is filled in.
