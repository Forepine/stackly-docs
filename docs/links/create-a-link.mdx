Paste a long link, give it a name, and get a short link you can share on WhatsApp, Instagram or a printed flyer.

<Shot id="links/create-form" title="The Create a short link form with a destination filled in" />

## Create a link
1. In the left menu, click **Links**.
2. Click **New link**. The **Create a short link** form opens on the **Single** tab.
3. In **Destination URL**, paste the long link, for example `https://mybakery.in/diwali-sweets`. It must start with `https://` or `http://`.
4. Wait a few seconds. Stackly fills in **Title** from the page. Change it if you like.
5. Optional: type your own back-half in **Short link** (see below).
6. Optional: add **Tags**.
7. Click **Create link**.

## Choose your own back-half
The back-half is the part after the slash: in `stackly.in/diwali-sweets`, it's `diwali-sweets`.
- Type it in **Short link**. Leave it empty and Stackly makes one for you.
- Use letters, numbers, hyphens (`-`) and underscores (`_`) only.
- Each back-half can be used only once. If it's taken, you'll see a message asking you to try another.

## Pick a domain
The box in front of **Short link** shows the domain, **stackly.in** by default.
1. Click the domain box.
2. Pick one of your own domains, for example `go.mybakery.in`.

Only verified domains show up here. To add one, see [custom domains](../workspace/custom-domains).

## Add a title and tags
- **Title** is required (up to 300 characters). It's how you find the link later, so make it clear: "Diwali sweets – Instagram bio".
- **Tags** help you group links. Type a tag and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or a comma. Click the **×** on a tag to remove it.

On a large screen, the **Live preview** on the right shows the short link, title, tags and settings as you type.

<Shot id="links/live-preview" title="Live preview panel next to the form" />

## Copy and share your new link
After you click **Create link**, you're back on the **Links** page and the new link is in the list.
1. Click the copy icon next to the short link.
2. Paste it anywhere: WhatsApp, email, your Instagram bio.

Every link also gets a QR code. See [QR codes](./qr-codes).

<Shot id="links/new-link-in-list" title="A new link in the Links list with the copy icon" />

## Good to know
- You can't change the **Destination URL** after the link is created. Make a new link instead.
- For password, expiry, targeting and UTM tags, open **More options**. See [link options](./link-options).
- Need many links at once? Use the **Bulk** tab. See [shorten many links at once](./bulk-links).
- Your plan sets how many links you can create. See [plans and limits](../getting-started/plans-and-limits).

## Troubleshooting
- **"Title is required"** → Type a title, or wait for Stackly to fetch it.
- **"Already in use"** → That back-half is taken. Try another, or leave it empty.
- **"You have reached the maximum short link limit"** → Upgrade your plan to create more.
- **New link is greyed out** → Your free trial has ended. See [manage your links](./manage-links#if-your-free-trial-has-ended).
