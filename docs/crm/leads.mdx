Leads is your contact list. Stackly fills it for you as people use your pages, and you can add contacts yourself.

## Where leads come from
- **Event RSVPs**: every guest who RSVPs to one of your [event pages](../grow/event-pages) is saved, tagged `event`.
- **Gated vCard links**: turn on **Require Email to Unlock (Lead Gate)** on a link in your [visiting card](../visiting-cards). Visitors type their name and email to open it, and each one becomes a lead.
- **Brand deals**: when a brand sends a request from your vCard, or you add a [brand deal](./brand-deals) yourself, the contact is saved here too.
- **You**: add someone by hand with **Add lead**.

If the same email comes in again, Stackly updates the lead it already has and adds the new tags. You don't get a duplicate.

## Add a lead
1. In the left menu, under **CRM**, click **Leads**.
2. Click **Add lead**.
3. Type the **Email Address**. It's the only required field. Stackly guesses the name and company from the email.
4. Fill in **Full Name**, **Phone (Optional)** and **Source / Organization** if you like.
5. Under **Tags & Labels**, click presets such as **Brand Sponsor** or **Client**, or type your own tags, separated by commas.
6. Click **Save Lead Contact**.

:::tip
Got an email signature or a WhatsApp message with someone's details? Click **Paste Contact Text**, paste it in, then click **Auto-Fill Lead Fields**.
:::

## Find a lead
- Type in the box **Search name, email, resource or tag**.
- Switch between the table and the card view with the view buttons at the top.
- The table shows **Contact**, **Source**, **Tags**, **Location** and **Date**.

## Edit a lead
1. Click the pencil (**Edit lead**) on the lead.
2. Change the email, name, phone, **Source / Resource Note** or **Tags (comma separated)**.
3. Click **Update Lead**.

## Export your leads
1. Click **Export CSV**.
2. Stackly downloads a file with the name, email, phone, source, tags, country, city and date of every lead. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets.

## Delete a lead
1. Click the bin (**Delete lead**) on the lead.
2. Confirm in the window that opens.

:::caution
A deleted lead can't be brought back.
:::

## Good to know
- Leads needs the Growth plan or higher. On Free and Starter the page shows an upgrade screen, and the menu shows a **Pro** badge.
- Leads have no status or notes. Use tags to sort them, for example `Hot` or `Pune`. To track stages and money, use [Brand deals](./brand-deals).
- You can't import a file of leads yet. Add them one at a time.
- Location (city, country) comes from the visitor's connection, so contacts you add yourself have none.
