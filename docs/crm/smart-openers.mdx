---
title: Smart openers
description: Stackly short links to YouTube, Instagram, X and other apps open straight in the app on phones, not in a logged-out browser.
---

When someone taps a YouTube or Instagram link inside another app, it often opens in a small browser where they aren't logged in. They can't subscribe, follow or buy in one tap, so many give up. Smart openers fix this: on a phone, your Stackly short link opens the real app instead.

<Shot id="crm/smart-opener-phone" title="Phone screen showing 'Opening YouTube' with the Open in YouTube and Continue in browser buttons" device="phone" />

## When to use it
- A creator puts a YouTube video link in an Instagram bio, so fans land in the YouTube app, already logged in, one tap from **Subscribe**.
- A shop shares an Amazon product link on WhatsApp, so buyers open the Amazon app with their cart and saved address.

## Supported apps

| App | Links that open in the app |
|---|---|
| YouTube | Videos, Shorts, channels |
| Instagram | Posts, Reels, profiles |
| X (Twitter) | Posts, profiles |
| Spotify | Tracks, albums, playlists and more |
| Amazon | Products and store pages |
| LinkedIn | Profiles and posts |
| Telegram | Channels and users (`t.me` links) |

## Create a smart opener
There's nothing to switch on.
1. [Create a short link](../links/create-a-link) to a page on one of the apps above.
2. Share the short link as normal.

When someone opens it on an iPhone or Android phone, they see a short **Opening…** screen and the app starts. If the app isn't installed, the page opens in the browser instead. They can also tap **Continue in browser**. On a computer, the link opens the page as usual.

## Check a link before you share it
1. Go to the **Smart App Openers & Deep Links** page (`/crm/smart-openers` in Stackly).
2. Paste your link into **Target Destination URL**, or click an example under **Example Presets**.
3. Read **Detected Platform** to see which app it will open.
4. Click **Open Web URL** to check the page itself.

<Shot id="crm/smart-opener-inspector" title="Smart App Openers page with a YouTube link pasted and Detected Platform shown" />

## Good to know
- Smart opening works on short links on every plan.
- The checker page needs the Growth plan or higher.
- Smart openers aren't used on [event pages](../grow/event-pages) or links with a password.

## Troubleshooting
- **The link opened the browser, not the app** → The app may not be installed, or the link points to a page type the app can't open (for example Instagram Explore). Check it on the checker page.
