Brand deals is a simple pipeline for creators. Each deal is one brand, one budget and one stage, so you can see what's waiting, what's in talks and what you've won.

## Where deals come from
- **Your vCard**: turn on **Show Brand Deals & Inquiries Button** in your [visiting card](../visiting-cards). Brands fill in a short form and the request lands here as **New**.
- **You**: add a deal you got by email or DM with **Add deal**.

Each deal's contact is also saved in [Leads](./leads).

## Add a deal
1. In the left menu, under **CRM**, click **Brand Deals**.
2. Click **Add deal**.
3. Type the **Work Email** and **Brand Name**. Both are required.
4. Add the **Contact Person** and **Phone** if you have them.
5. Under **Deal Budget**, pick **USD ($)** or **INR (₹)** and type the amount.
6. Pick a stage, for example **New Lead** or **In Discussion**.
7. Click deliverable presets such as **Dedicated Reel** or **YouTube Short**, or type your own.
8. Add a **Timeline / Target Date**, **Internal CRM Notes** and the **Campaign Brief / Message** if you like.
9. Click **Save Brand Deal**.

:::tip
Paste the brand's email into **Smart Auto-Extract from Email / Pitch** and click **Auto-Fill Form Fields**. Stackly fills in the brand, contact and budget for you.
:::

## Move a deal to the next stage
1. On the deal, click its coloured status badge.
2. Pick the new stage. It saves straight away.

| Stage | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **New** | The request just came in. |
| **Contacted** | You've replied. |
| **In discussion** | You're agreeing rates and dates. |
| **Won** | The deal is signed. |
| **Lost** | The brand passed, or you did. |

## Add notes or edit a deal
1. Click the deal's name to open it.
2. Type in **Internal CRM Notes**, then click **Save Notes**. Only you see these.
3. To change anything else, click the pencil (**Edit deal**), make your changes and click **Update Deal**.

## Find deals
- Use the tabs (**All**, **New**, **Contacted**, **In discussion**, **Won**, **Lost**) to filter by stage.
- Type in **Search brand, contact or brief**.
- Switch between cards and table with the view buttons.
- Click **Export CSV** to download every deal.

## Delete a deal
1. Click the bin (**Delete deal**).
2. Confirm.

:::caution
A deleted deal can't be brought back.
:::

## Good to know
- Brand deals needs the Growth plan or higher. On Free and Starter you see an upgrade screen.
- **Pipeline value** adds up deals that aren't lost. **Won value** adds up won deals. USD and INR are shown separately, never added together.
- Dates are free text (for example "Q4 launch"), not a calendar.
