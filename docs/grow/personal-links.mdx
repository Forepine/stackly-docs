A personal link list gives each person their own short link to the same page, like `stackly.in/p/aB3x9`. When someone clicks, you know it was them.

<Shot id="personal-links/list-detail" title="A personal link list showing who Clicked and who is Not yet" />

## When to use it
- **Online stores:** see which VIP customers opened your WhatsApp offer, then re-send to "Not yet" the next day.
- **Real estate and insurance agents:** call the leads who actually opened your brochure first.
- **Agencies and freelancers:** know when a client opened your proposal before you follow up.
- **Schools and coaching institutes:** make sure every parent saw the fee or exam notice.
- **Event organisers:** see who opened your invite, not just who registered.
- **Recruiters:** follow up with candidates who read the job description.

## Create a list
1. In the left menu, under **Grow**, click **Personal Links**.
2. Click **New list**. Or pick an idea, such as **Online stores & D2C brands**, to fill in the message and tags for you.
   <Shot id="personal-links/new-list" title="New personal link list form with message, UTM fields and People box" />
3. Type a **List name** and the **Link to send**.
4. Check **utm_source** and **utm_medium** (for example `whatsapp` and `message`). The campaign tag is set from the list name for you.
5. Write the **Message**. `{name}` becomes the person's first name and `{link}` their own link.
6. Under **People**, paste one person per line (name, phone, email), or click **Upload CSV**.
7. Check the count and the preview, then click **Create _N_ links** (for example **Create 40 links**).

## See who clicked
Open a list to see:
- **People**, **Clicked**, **Not yet** and **Click rate**
- tabs to show **All**, **Clicked** or **Not yet**, and a search box
- for each person who clicked: when they first clicked, how many times they opened it, and their city and device

## Send the links
On each person's row:
- **WhatsApp** opens a chat with your message filled in (shown when they have a phone number).
- **Email** opens a new email with your message (shown when they have an email).
- Click their link to copy it, or **Message** to copy the full message.

## Export, turn off or delete a list
- Click **CSV** to download everyone with their link, clicks, opens, first and last click, city and device.
- Use the **Live** / **Off** switch to pause the whole list. While it's off, every link shows "This personal link is paused".
- Click the bin button to delete the list.

:::caution
Deleting a list deletes all its links. Anyone who opens one sees "link not found". This can't be undone.
:::

## Good to know
- Each person uses one short link from your plan. The form shows how many you have left. Deleting a list gives them back.
- Up to 500 people per list. CSV files must be under 2 MB.
- Links stop working 30 days after you create them.
- Phone numbers with 10 digits are treated as Indian (+91) for WhatsApp.
- When a free trial ends, a banner says **Your free trial has ended.** Your lists keep working, but you need a paid plan to create new ones.

## Troubleshooting
- **No WhatsApp button for someone** → Their line had no phone number. Add it and make a new list.
- **"Not enough links left on your plan"** → Send to fewer people or [upgrade your plan](../account/billing-and-plans).
- **Some lines were skipped** → Each line needs at least a name, phone or email.
