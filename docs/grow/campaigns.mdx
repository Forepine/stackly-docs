A campaign is one offer (one destination page) with its own short link for each place you share it: Instagram, WhatsApp, email, ads and more. Stackly adds the UTM tags for you and adds up the clicks for the whole campaign.

<Shot id="campaigns/list" title="Campaigns page with campaign cards and the status filter" />

## When to use it
- A bakery posts its Diwali sale on Instagram, a WhatsApp broadcast and an email newsletter. Each gets its own link, so the owner sees which one brought the most visitors.

## Create a campaign <PlanBadge plan="Growth" />
1. In the left menu, under **Grow**, click **Campaigns**.
2. Click **Create Campaign**. Or, on an empty page, pick a ready-made plan under **Or Launch From A Campaign Blueprint** and click **Use this blueprint**.
3. Under **Campaign details**, enter a **Campaign name** (for example "Diwali Sale 2026") and the **Destination URL**, the page people should land on.
4. Optional: choose a **Short link domain**, pick **Starts** and **Ends** dates, and add a **Description** and **Tags**.
5. Click **Next: choose channels**.
6. Click each channel you use: **Twitter / X**, **Facebook**, **LinkedIn**, **Instagram**, **YouTube**, **Email Newsletter**, **SMS Marketing** or **WhatsApp**. For anything else, click **Custom**.
   <Shot id="campaigns/new-form" title="New campaign form with channels selected and the link preview for each" />
7. Click **Launch campaign**.

Each channel now has its own short link with the campaign name, source and medium already tagged.

## Use the campaign page
Click a campaign card to open it. You will see:
- **Clicks**, **Channels**, **Top channel** and the dates.
- A status switch: **Active**, **Paused**, **Completed** or **Draft**. This label helps you sort your list.
- The **Channels** tab: each channel's short link, its clicks and its share of the total. Use the buttons to copy the link, show or download the QR code, or open the link.

## Compare channels
1. On the campaign page, click **Analytics**.
2. Pick a time range: **Last 7 Days**, **Last 14 Days**, **Last 30 Days**, **Last 90 Days** or **All Time**.
3. Read the cards: **Clicks**, **Channels**, **Top channel** and **Avg per day**.
4. Check **Channel Performance Comparison** to see which channel won. You also get **Clicks Over Time**, **Geographic Distribution**, **Top Referrers**, **Devices**, **Browsers** and **Operating systems**.
   <Shot id="campaigns/analytics" title="Campaign analytics with the channel comparison chart" />

To send these results to a client, click **Share report**.

## Add a channel
1. Open the campaign and click **Add channel**.
2. Pick from **Quick pick** (this list also has **TikTok** and **Google Ads**), or type a **Channel name**, **utm_source** and **utm_medium**.
3. Optional: change the **Destination URL**, **Short link domain** or **Custom slug** (the part after the slash).
4. Click **Add channel**.

To remove one, click the bin icon on its row. Its short link stops being counted in this campaign.

## Edit a campaign
1. Open the campaign and click the **Settings** tab (or click the pencil on the card).
2. Change the details and click **Save changes**.

## Delete a campaign
1. On the **Campaigns** page, click the bin icon on the campaign's card.
2. Confirm.

:::caution
You can't undo deleting a campaign.
:::

## Good to know
- Campaigns are on **Growth** and higher plans. On Free and Starter, the menu shows a **Pro** badge and the page asks you to upgrade.
- Each campaign name must be different from your other campaign names.
- Use the tabs **All**, **Active**, **Paused**, **Completed** and **Draft**, or **Search campaigns**, to find one fast.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Campaign with name … already exists"** → Pick a different name, for example add the year.
