An event page is one short link, like `stackly.in/event/diwali-tasting-k7x2`. It opens a page with the date, a countdown, a sign-up form, **Add to calendar**, a map for venues and share buttons.

<Shot id="events/public-page" title="A public event page on a phone: cover, date, countdown and Register button" device="phone" />

## When to use it
- A bakery in Pune shares its Diwali tasting on WhatsApp.
- A yoga studio runs a workshop with 20 spots and a waitlist.
- An agency sends the webinar's Zoom link only to people who register.

## Create an event page
1. In the left menu, under **Grow**, click **Event Pages**, then **New event**.
   <Shot id="events/new-form" title="The New event page form with cover, name, dates and Event options" />
2. **Cover:** click the picture button to upload a JPG, PNG or WebP (under 5 MB). Or click the link button, paste an image link (an Unsplash page works) and click **Use**.
3. Type the **Event name**.
4. Pick **Start** and **End**. Moving the start moves the end too, so the length stays the same.
5. Click the time zone box to change it. Search any time zone by city, country or offset, such as `GMT+5:30`.
6. Click **Add event location** and type one of these:
   - a venue address (guests get an **Open in Maps** link)
   - a Google Maps link (guests see the place on a map)
   - a Zoom, Meet, Teams or YouTube link (guests get it after they register)
7. Click **Add description**.
8. Click **Theme** and pick one of 17 themes in **Simple**, **Bold**, **Celebrate**, **Nature** and **Pattern** (or click shuffle). Then pick a **Color** and a title **Font**.
9. In the top-right menu, choose **Public** or **Private**.
10. Set the **Event options** below, then click **Create event page**.

### Event options

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Helps people find public events on Discover. |
| **Registration** | Guests leave their name and email. Turn off if no sign-up is needed. |
| **Require approval** | You approve each guest. |
| **Capacity** | **Unlimited**, or a number. When full, new guests join a waitlist. |
| **Ask for phone (WhatsApp)** | Guests must add a phone number. |
| **Show who's going** | Shows first names and initials of guests on the page, like "Riya, Kabir and 16 others". |
| **Registration questions** | Your own questions, such as Instagram ID, company or T-shirt size. See [registration questions](./event-guests#ask-guests-your-own-questions). |
| **Hosted by** | Add co-hosts or partners. The first name is the main host. |
| **Button** | A button to tickets, a store or a landing page, with your own text. |
| **Event link** | Pick your [custom domain](../workspace/custom-domains) and the part after `/event/`. Leave it empty to make one from the name. |
| **Hide "Made with Stackly"** | Removes the Stackly badge. <PlanBadge plan="Growth" /> |

**Public** events are listed on Discover and can show up in Google. **Private** events are unlisted: only people with the link can see them and register.

For approvals and the waitlist, see [Manage event guests](./event-guests).

## Find your events
- Switch between **Upcoming** and **Past**, or use **Search events**.
- Rows show the date, place, how many are going, and labels like **Off**, **Private**, **pending** or **Full**.
- On plans with a limit, you see **X of N used** next to **New event**.

## Manage an event
Click an event to open its tabs:
- **Overview:** **Invite on WhatsApp**, **Copy event link**, **QR code** (**Download PNG**), **Edit event**, **Change photo**, and share to WhatsApp, Facebook, X or LinkedIn.
- **Guests:** your guest list.
- **Insights:** page views for the past 7 or 30 days, registrations, and visitors by city, device and browser.
- **More:** **Clone event** (copies everything except guests), change the **Event link**, **Embed a register button** for your website, and **Delete event**.

<Shot id="events/detail-overview" title="Event Overview tab with share buttons, QR code and When & Where" />

:::caution
Changing the event link breaks the old one. Deleting an event removes the page, its link and the guest list, and can't be undone. Guests stay in your CRM leads.
:::

## Turn an event off
Use the **Live** / **Off** switch at the top of the event. While it's **Off**, the link shows "*Your event* isn't open right now", new RSVPs are closed and the event is hidden from Discover. Guests who already registered keep their spot.

## Get found on Discover
[stackly.in/discover](https://www.stackly.in/discover) lists upcoming events that are **Public** and **Live**, by category, such as **Food & Drink** or **Fitness & Wellness**. Pick a **Category** so yours shows in the right place.

## Good to know
- **Free plan:** 2 event pages in total. Deleting one doesn't free a slot.
- When a free trial ends, a banner says **Your free trial has ended.** Existing events keep working, but you need a paid plan to create new ones.
- The event link works for 30 days after the event ends.

## Troubleshooting
- **New event is greyed out** → You've hit your plan's limit or your trial ended. Click **Upgrade plan**.
- **No map on the page** → Use a Google Maps link as the location.
- **Not on Discover** → Check the event is **Public**, **Live** and hasn't ended.
