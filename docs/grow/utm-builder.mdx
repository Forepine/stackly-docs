UTM tags are small labels added to the end of a link. They tell Google Analytics (and other tools) where a visitor came from. Stackly lets you save them as templates and add them to any short link in one click.

## What the tags mean

| Tag | What it says | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Source (`utm_source`) | Which site or app sent the visitor | `instagram`, `google`, `newsletter` |
| Medium (`utm_medium`) | What kind of traffic it is | `social`, `cpc`, `email` |
| Campaign (`utm_campaign`) | Which offer or launch it belongs to | `diwali_sale_2026` |
| Term (`utm_term`) | The paid search keyword | `running+shoes` |
| Content (`utm_content`) | Which ad or link version | `banner_v2` |
| Referral (`utm_ref`) | A partner or referrer id | `partner_portal` |

A yoga studio that tags its Instagram bio link with `utm_source=instagram` can later see in Google Analytics exactly how many bookings came from Instagram.

## Create a UTM template <PlanBadge plan="Starter" />
1. In the left menu, under **Grow**, click **UTM Builder**.
2. Click **New template**. To start from a common setup, click **Presets**, pick one and change what you need.
3. Under **Template**, enter a **Name** (for example "Instagram paid") and, if you like, a **Description**.
4. Under **Tags**, fill in **Source** (required), then **Medium** and **Campaign**. **Term**, **Content** and **Referral** are optional.
5. Check the **Preview**. Paste a page into **Try it on a URL** to see the full tagged link, then click **Copy tags** or **Copy URL** if you need it now.
   <Shot id="utm/new-template" title="New UTM template form with tags filled in and the live preview" />
6. Click **Create template**.

Templates are shared across your workspace, so everyone on your team picks the same names.

:::tip
Use lowercase and underscores. Google Analytics treats `Facebook` and `facebook` as two different sources.
:::

## Use a template on a link
1. Create a link as usual and enter the destination.
2. Open **UTM tracking** and click **Choose a template or add UTM tags**.
3. In **Load Saved Preset Template**, pick your template. The tags fill in for you.
   <Shot id="utm/apply-on-link" title="UTM Campaign Builder window on the link form with a saved template chosen" />
4. Change any tag if needed, then click **Apply Parameters**.

You can also type tags here without a template. **Source**, **Medium** and **Campaign** are required in this window.

## Edit or delete a template
1. Open **UTM Builder**.
2. On the template card, click the pencil to edit, then **Save changes**. Or click the bin icon to delete it and confirm.

The copy icon on a card copies just the tags (the part starting `?utm_`).

## Free UTM template gallery
Anyone can browse ready-made tag setups at [stackly.in/utm-templates](https://stackly.in/utm-templates), with no account. It covers Meta Ads, Google Ads, Instagram creators, WhatsApp, email newsletters, YouTube, LinkedIn, QR codes on print, and SMS. Each page lets you paste your page under **Landing page** and copy the tagged link.

## Good to know
- Saving templates and adding UTM tags to links needs **Starter** or above. On Free, the menu shows a **Starter** badge. You can try the builder and browse presets, but the save button reads **Upgrade to save**.
- Each template name must be unique in your workspace.
- [Campaigns](./campaigns) add these tags for you, one link per channel.
