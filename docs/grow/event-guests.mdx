Everyone who registers on your [event page](./event-pages) shows up in the **Guests** tab. Here you approve people, let them in from the waitlist and download the list.

<Shot id="events/guests-tab" title="Guests tab with At a glance bar and a guest list showing Going, Pending and Waitlist" />

## Guest statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Going** | They're in. They got a confirmation email. |
| **Pending** | **Require approval** is on and they're waiting for you. |
| **Waitlist** | The event was full when they registered. |
| **Declined** | You declined or removed them. |

## How guests get a status
- **Open event:** guests are **Going** straight away while there's room.
- **Require approval on:** every new guest starts as **Pending**, and the button on the page says **Request to join**.
- **Capacity reached:** new guests go on the **Waitlist**, and the button says **Join waitlist**. People on the waitlist aren't moved up by themselves. You let them in.
- Each email can register once. If someone registers again with the same email, they keep the status they had.

## Approve or decline guests
1. Open the event and click the **Guests** tab. A number on the tab shows how many are pending.
2. Use the filter to show **Pending** or **Waitlist** guests.
3. Click **Approve** to let someone in, or **Decline** to say no.
4. To change your mind later, click **Remove** on a **Going** guest, or **Let in** on a **Declined** guest.

:::note
You can approve a guest even when the event is full, so the count can go above your capacity.
:::

## Emails guests get

| When | Subject |
|---|---|
| They register and are in straight away | You're registered: *event name* |
| You approve them | You're in! *event name* |
| You decline or remove them | Update on your registration: *event name* |

Going guests get the date, place, **Add to Google Calendar** and an **Apple / Outlook (.ics)** file. For online events, the email has the joining link. Pending and waitlisted guests get no email until you decide.

## What guests see when they come back
The page remembers the guest on their device and shows their status:
- **You're in**, with the joining link for online events
- **Pending approval**: "Waiting for the host"
- **Waitlisted**: "You're on the waitlist"
- **Declined**: "The host couldn't fit you in"

Someone else on the same phone can click **Not you? Register with another email**.

## Download the guest list
1. In the **Guests** tab, click the download button next to **Guest list**.
2. You get a CSV file with name, email, phone, status, country and registration time.

## Good to know
- Every guest is also added to your [CRM Leads](../crm/leads), tagged with the event.
- Search the list by name, email or phone, and sort by **Newest first** or **Oldest first**.
- Deleting the event deletes the guest list, but the guests stay in Leads.

## Troubleshooting
- **A guest didn't get an email** → Ask them to check spam. Pending and waitlisted guests only get one after you approve or decline them.
- **A guest can't register** → The event may be **Off**, or **Registration** is turned off.
