The audit log is a diary of your workspace. It lists each action, such as a link made, a member invited or a domain added, with who did it and when. It helps when several people share one account, like an agency team or a shop with a few staff.

<Shot id="account/audit-logs-list" title="Workspace Activity list with category badges, names and times" />

## When to use it
- A link changed and you want to know who changed it.
- You want to check what a new team member has been doing.
- You see a sign-in or setting change you don't recognise.

## Open the audit log
1. Click your name at the bottom of the left menu.
2. Click **Preferences**.
3. In the settings menu, click **Audit Logs**. The page is called **Workspace Activity**.

Each row shows:
- What happened, with a category badge and a **Success** or **Failed** label.
- Who did it (name and email).
- Their browser or device and IP address.
- The date and time.

## Filter the list
1. Click **All Categories**.
2. Pick a category: **Links**, **Bio Pages**, **Campaigns**, **Analytics**, **Domains**, **Integrations**, **Team & Access**, **Billing**, **Account** or **Security**.
3. To see everything again, click **Clear filter**.

You can also type in the search box at the top of the page to find a person, email, action or IP address.

## See more detail
1. Click a row to open it.
2. Under **Technical Details** you'll see the **Browser / Device** and **Hostname**. If the action failed, the **Error Message** is shown too.
   <Shot id="account/audit-logs-details" title="An opened audit log row showing Technical Details" />

## Good to know
- The account Owner sees everyone's actions. Other members see only their own.
- Actions are kept for 21 days, then removed.
