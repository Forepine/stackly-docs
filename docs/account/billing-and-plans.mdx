The **Billing** page shows your plan, what you've used this month and your past payments. Open it from the account menu at the bottom of the left menu: **Billing & Plans**. You can also click the plan chip at the top of the screen.

<Shot id="account/billing-page" title="The Billing page with plan, clicks this cycle and usage" />

## Check your plan and usage
- **Your plan:** name, status (**Active**, **Trial** or **Free**), price per month, **Billed monthly** or **Billed yearly**, and the **Renews** date.
- **Clicks this cycle:** clicks used out of your limit, and how many are left.
- **Usage:** a box for each limit (**Short URLs**, **Digital vCards**, **Custom Domains**, **Stack Notes**, **Team Members** and more) with **% used** and what's left. Monthly limits reset on your renewal date.

For what each limit means, see [plans and limits](../getting-started/plans-and-limits).

## Upgrade or change your plan
Stackly doesn't charge your card on this screen. You send a request, and the Stackly team gets in touch to complete payment and switch on your plan.

1. Click **Upgrade plan** (or **Change plan** if you're already paying).
2. At the top, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly — Save 2 months**.
   - **Yearly** shows the full yearly price in large type, with the per-month price and your saving underneath. You pay for 12 months upfront.
   - **Monthly** shows the price per month.
3. Click a plan card to select it. Scroll down to **Compare plans** to see limits side by side.
4. Click **Get** and the plan name (for example **Get Growth**). For Business, click **Contact sales**.
5. Check your **Name**, **Email** and **Mobile**, and add a **Note for our team** if you like.
6. Click **Request upgrade**.

<Shot id="account/upgrade-plans" title="The Plans page with the Monthly / Yearly toggle and plan cards" />

You'll see **Request sent**. The team emails you to finish payment, then activates your plan.

## Buy more clicks (top-ups)
A top-up is a one-time pack of extra clicks for links or visiting cards that are getting a lot of visits. Your plan stays the same.

1. Click **Buy clicks**.
2. Pick a pack, then click **Request this pack**.
3. Under **What should get the clicks?**, click **Browse links & vCards** and choose them.
4. Fill in your contact details and click **Request top-up**.

Clicks are added once the team confirms your payment.

## See and download invoices
1. Click **Invoices** (or **All invoices** under **Recent payments**).
2. Switch between **Plan** and **Top-ups**.
3. Click the eye icon to view an invoice, or the download icon to save the PDF.

**Invoice pending** means the payment is confirmed but the invoice isn't ready yet.

## Add billing details and GST
1. Open **Preferences** from the account menu, then click **Account**.
2. Click **Edit Details**, then open the **Billing & Tax** tab.
3. Fill in **Billing Name** (your company or your own name) and **Tax Details**: choose **GST (India)** or **VAT (International)** and type your number.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

<Shot id="account/billing-tax" title="The Billing & Tax tab on the Account page" />

Your invoices show the **Billing Name** you save here.

## Good to know
- You can have one open upgrade request and one open top-up request at a time.
- Need custom limits? Click **Talk to sales**.

## Troubleshooting
- **"You have already sent a … request"** → The team is already working on it. Wait for their email.
- **My plan hasn't changed after paying** → Plans switch on once the team confirms payment. Refresh the page, or reply to their email.
