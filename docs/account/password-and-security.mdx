---
title: Password and security
description: Change your password, add a password to a Google or GitHub account, sign out and delete your Stackly account.
---

Keep your account safe with a strong password, and sign out on shared computers. All of this lives in **Preferences** (the account menu at the bottom of the left menu).

## Change your password
1. Open **Preferences**. You land on **Profile**.
2. In the **Security** box, click **Change Password**.
3. Type a **New Password**, then type it again in **Confirm New Password**.
4. Click **Update Password**.

<Shot id="account/change-password" title="The Security box on Profile with the Change Password form open" />

Use at least 8 characters, with a capital letter and a number. You don't need your old password, so never leave Stackly open on a shared computer.

## Add a password if you signed up with Google or GitHub
Accounts made with **Google** or **GitHub** have no password at first. To also sign in with your email and a password:

1. Sign in with **Google** or **GitHub**.
2. Follow the steps in [change your password](#change-your-password) above.

From then on you can sign in either way. If you can't sign in at all, use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page to set one by email. See [create your account](../getting-started/create-your-account#reset-a-forgotten-password).

## Sign out
1. Click your name at the bottom of the left menu.
2. Click **Log Out**.

If you ticked **Keep me signed in**, you stay signed in for 7 days. Otherwise it's 1 day.

## Check who did what
You can see a record of actions in the workspace under **Preferences** → **Audit Logs**. See [audit logs](./audit-logs).

## Delete your account
Only the account **Owner** can do this.

1. Open **Preferences** → **Account**.
2. Scroll to **Danger Zone** and click **Delete Account**.
3. Confirm in the window that opens.

<Shot id="account/delete-account" title="The Danger Zone with the Delete Account button" />

:::caution
This deletes the whole workspace: your team members' logins, links and visiting cards. It can't be undone, and your short links stop working.
:::

## Good to know
- Stackly has no list of signed-in devices or "sign out everywhere" button. If you think someone else knows your password, change it straight away.
- Team members who aren't the Owner see **Owner only** instead of the delete button.

## Troubleshooting
- **The password won't save** → The form accepts 6 characters, but Stackly needs at least 8. Make it longer.
- **"Passwords do not match"** → Type the same password in both boxes.
- **"It looks like you registered with Google or GitHub"** → You haven't added a password yet. Sign in with Google or GitHub, or use **Forgot password?**.
