Your profile holds your name and photo. Your **Account** page holds the contact and billing details for your workspace.

## Open Settings
1. Click your name at the bottom of the left menu.
2. Click **Preferences**.

You can also click your photo at the top right and choose **Settings**. The settings menu on the left has **Profile**, **Account**, **API Keys**, **Affiliates**, **Audit Logs** and **Data Retention**. On a phone, tap **Preferences** at the top to open this menu.

## Change your name
1. Open **Profile**.
2. Next to **Profile Information**, click **Edit**.
3. Change **Full Name**.
4. Click **Save**.

<Shot id="account/profile-page" title="The Profile page with photo, Profile Information and Security" />

Your **Email Address** can't be changed.

## Add or change your photo
1. On **Profile**, click **Upload Photo** and choose an image.
2. Drag or resize the circle to frame your face.
3. Click **Crop & Apply**.

To take your photo off, click **Remove**.

## Check your account status
The **Account Status** box on **Profile** shows your **Plan Tier**, **Status**, and **Email Verification** (**Verified** or **Pending**). If it says **Pending**, see [verify your email](../getting-started/create-your-account#verify-your-email).

## Add your phone, address and billing details
1. Open **Account**.
2. Click **Edit Details**.
3. On the **Contact** tab, fill in **Phone Number** (10 digits) and **Address** (street, city, state and country). Click **Auto-fill Location** to fill the address from where you are.
4. On the **Billing & Tax** tab, fill in **Billing Name** and **Tax Details** (**GST (India)** or **VAT (International)** and your number).
5. Click **Save Changes**.

<Shot id="account/account-page" title="The Account page with the Contact and Billing & Tax tabs" />

## Good to know
- All fields on **Account** are required before you can save, including the tax number.
- The **Account** page also shows your **Account ID** and **Role**. Quote your Account ID when you contact support.
- Your invoices use the **Billing Name** from here. See [billing and plans](./billing-and-plans).
- To change your password or delete your account, see [password and security](./password-and-security).

## Troubleshooting
- **"Phone number must be exactly 10 digits"** → Type the number without the country code or spaces, like `9876543210`.
- **Auto-fill Location doesn't work** → Allow location access in your browser, or type the address yourself.
