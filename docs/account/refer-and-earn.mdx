Tell other shop owners, creators or clients about Stackly with your own referral link. You get ₹1 credit when someone signs up through it, and ₹5 when they buy their first paid plan. Credit isn't paid out in cash: it comes off your next Stackly plan.

<Shot id="account/referral-page" title="Affiliate Program page with the Referral Program card, code and link" />

## Find your referral link
1. Click your name at the bottom of the left menu.
2. Click **Preferences**.
3. In the settings menu, click **Affiliates**.
4. At the top, the **Referral Program** card shows your **Code** (it starts with `stly`) and your signup link.

## Share it
1. Click **Copy Link**.
2. Paste it wherever your audience is: WhatsApp, Instagram bio, a newsletter or your website.

The link opens the Stackly sign-up page with your code already added. Your friend can sign up with email, Google or GitHub; all of them count. You can also click **Copy Code**, but your friend needs to open the link for it to count.

## How rewards work

| When | You get (India) | You get (elsewhere) |
|---|---|---|
| Someone signs up through your link | ₹1 | $0.011 |
| They buy their first paid plan (once; renewals and later upgrades don't add more) | ₹5 | $0.057 |

Amounts show in rupees in India and in US dollars everywhere else. The dollar value is the same reward converted at ₹88 to $1, fixed on the day you earn it.

A sign-up reward shows as **Pending** until your friend verifies their email. Google and GitHub sign-ups are verified already, so they count straight away.

## Use your credit
Your credit comes off automatically when you request a plan:
1. Go to **Billing** and pick a plan.
2. The upgrade form shows your credit, for example **−₹23 referral credit · you pay ₹4,967 for the year**.
3. Click **Request upgrade**. The request goes to our team with the reduced amount, and that's what you're invoiced.

Credit used on a request can't be used again. If you have more credit than the plan costs, the plan is free and the rest stays for next time.

## Track your referrals
On the **Affiliates** page:
- **Available credit** is what comes off your next plan (everything confirmed, less what you've already used).
- **Pending** is sign-up rewards waiting for friends to verify their email.
- **Friends joined** counts people who signed up with your link, and **Upgraded** how many of them bought a plan.
- The list shows each reward, newest first: your friend's email (partly hidden, like `ra***@gmail.com`), whether they signed up or paid, the date and the amount.

## Good to know
- Only brand-new accounts count. Someone who already has a Stackly account can't be referred.
- You can't refer yourself.
- Each friend earns you the plan reward once, on their first paid plan.
- If you have no referrals yet, you'll see **No referrals yet**.

## Troubleshooting
- **A friend signed up but isn't in my list** → They may have opened Stackly without your link. Ask them to use your exact link next time.
- **A reward says Pending** → Your friend hasn't clicked the link in their verification email yet.
