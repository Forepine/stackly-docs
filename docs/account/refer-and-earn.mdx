Tell other shop owners, creators or clients about Stackly with your own referral link. When someone signs up through it, you earn a reward, and you earn again if they buy a paid plan.

<Shot id="account/referral-page" title="Affiliate Program page with the Referral Program card, code and link" />

## Find your referral link
1. Click your name at the bottom of the left menu.
2. Click **Preferences**.
3. In the settings menu, click **Affiliates**.
4. At the top, the **Referral Program** card shows your **Code** (it starts with `stly`) and your signup link.

## Share it
1. Click **Copy Link**.
2. Paste it wherever your audience is: WhatsApp, Instagram bio, a newsletter or your website.

The link opens the Stackly sign-up page with your code already added. You can also click **Copy Code**, but your friend needs to sign up through the link for it to count.

## How rewards work

| When | You get |
|---|---|
| Someone signs up through your link | A sign-up reward |
| That person buys a paid plan | A subscription reward |

Each reward shows as a row in your list, with the amount under **Earning** and the type under **Commission** (for example **Sign-up**).

## Track your referrals
On the **Affiliates** page:
- **Total Revenue** shows what you've earned, in US dollars.
- **Active Affiliates** counts the rewards in your list. A friend who signs up and then upgrades counts twice.
- The list below shows each referral's email, **Earning** and **Commission** type.

<Shot id="account/referral-list" title="Referral list with Total Revenue, Active Affiliates and one referral row" />

## Good to know
- Your friend must create their account using the email sign-up form after opening your link.
- If you have no referrals yet, you'll see **No Affiliates Yet**.

## Troubleshooting
- **A friend signed up but isn't in my list** → They may have opened Stackly without your link, or signed up with Google. Ask them to use your exact link next time.
