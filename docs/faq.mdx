Quick answers. Each one links to the full guide.

## Account and plans

**Is there a free plan?**
Yes. Every account starts on **Free** with a 7-day trial. See [plans and limits](./getting-started/plans-and-limits).

**What happens when my trial ends?**
Everything you made keeps working, but you can't create new links, event pages or personal links until you upgrade. See [your free trial](./getting-started/plans-and-limits#your-free-trial).

**How do I pay or upgrade?**
Go to **Billing & Plans** → **Upgrade plan**, pick a plan and send the request. Our team confirms it and your plan changes. See [billing and plans](./account/billing-and-plans).

**Can my team use the same account?**
Yes. Invite people from **Team** and give each one a role. See [team members](./account/team-members).

**I didn't get the verification email.**
Check your spam folder, then click **Resend email**. Signing in with Google or GitHub skips this step. See [create your account](./getting-started/create-your-account).

## Links

**Can I choose what comes after the slash?**
Yes. Type your own back-half when you create the link, for example `stackly.in/diwali-sale`. See [create a short link](./links/create-a-link).

**Can I change where a link goes after I've shared it?**
No. A link's destination can't be changed. Create a new link instead. You can still edit its title, tags and options. See [manage your links](./links/manage-links).

**Do short links expire?**
They can. Set a date under **More options** → **Security** → **Expires on**. See [link options](./links/link-options).

**Someone says my link doesn't open.**
The page they see tells you why: turned off, expired, click limit reached, wrong device or country, or a domain that isn't set up yet. See [what visitors see](./links/what-visitors-see).

**Can I use my own domain, like go.mybrand.com?**
Yes, on Growth and higher plans. See [custom domains](./workspace/custom-domains).

**Where's the QR code for a link?**
Every link has one. You can download it as an image. See [QR codes](./links/qr-codes).

## Events and personal links

**How do guests register for my event?**
They open your event link, type their name and email and click **Register**. You don't need to send them a form. See [event pages](./grow/event-pages).

**Can I approve guests before they join?**
Yes. Turn on **Require approval**, then approve or decline people on the **Guests** tab. Guests get an email either way. See [manage event guests](./grow/event-guests).

**What's the difference between a short link and a personal link?**
A short link is the same for everyone. A personal link list gives each person their own link, so you can see exactly who opened it. See [personal links](./grow/personal-links).

## Analytics and reports

**What counts as a click?**
Each time someone opens your link. **Unique visitors** counts each person once, so one person clicking three times is three clicks but one visitor. See [analytics](./insights/analytics).

**Can my client see the numbers without a Stackly account?**
Yes. Share a read-only report link. See [share a report with a client](./insights/client-report-link).

**How long is click data kept?**
It depends on your plan. Older data moves to an archive for 60 days before it's deleted. See [data retention](./account/data-retention).
