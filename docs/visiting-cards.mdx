A digital visiting card (vCard) is a small web page about you or your business, at a link like `yourname.stackly.in`. People can call, WhatsApp or email you in one tap and save you to their phone contacts.

<Shot id="vcards/public-card" title="A public visiting card on a phone with photo, contact rows and the Share, QR Code and Save bar" device="phone" />

## When to use it
- A real estate agent shares a card instead of a paper visiting card.
- A café puts its card QR on the counter, with WhatsApp, map and menu links.
- A creator adds a **Brand Deals** button so brands can send sponsorship requests.

## Create a card
1. In the left menu, click **Visiting Card**.
2. Click **Create vCard** (or **Create Your First vCard**).
3. Go through the steps on the left. Click **Next Step** to move on. The **Live Preview** shows your card as you go.
   <Shot id="vcards/form" title="vCard form with the steps on the left and the live preview" />

| Step | What you add |
|---|---|
| **Template** | A starting design. Some templates are premium and need a paid plan. |
| **Identity** | Profile photo, **Cover Banner Image URL**, **Subdomain Handle / Slug** (required), **Full Name** (required) and **Bio & Short Headline**. You can also choose a [custom domain](./workspace/custom-domains). |
| **Contact & Work** | **Job Title / Designation**, **Company / Brand Name**, **Official Website**, **Mobile Phone Number**, **WhatsApp Number**, **Work Email Address** and **Office / Business Address**. |
| **Highlights** | A **Main call to action** button (WhatsApp, Call, Link or Email), **Trust stats** like "500+ Happy clients", and **Services & pricing**. |
| **Links & Promos** | Buttons and promo cards with a title, link, badge and image. Turn on **Require Email to Unlock (Lead Gate)** to ask for an email first. |
| **Socials** | Instagram, LinkedIn, X / Twitter, WhatsApp, Facebook, YouTube, GitHub, Telegram and TikTok. |
| **Appearance** | Layout (**Classic**, **Spotlight**, **Executive**, **Editorial**, **Glass**), button style, colours, font and photo shape. You can also turn **Show Brand Deals & Inquiries Button** on or off, and remove the watermark. |

4. Click **Publish Card**.

## What people see
- Your photo, name, title, company and bio
- Tap-to-contact rows: phone, **Chat on WhatsApp**, email, location and website
- Your links, promo cards and social icons under **Follow**
- A bar with **Share**, **QR Code** and **Save**. **Save** downloads a contact file (.vcf) that adds you to their phone contacts.
- If Brand Deals is on, a **Work with** *your name* form for sponsorship requests. These go to [Brand Deals](./crm/brand-deals).

## Share, edit and manage your card
Each card in the list has:
- buttons to copy the link, open the live card and share it
- a QR code to **Download** or **Share** (flip the card to see it)
- a view count, and an on/off switch. When it's off, the card doesn't open.
- **Pin to top** and **Turn on visit alerts**
- a menu with **Edit**, **Send**, **Move to Collection** and **Delete**

To change a card, click **Edit**, make your changes, then click **Save Changes**.

## Good to know
- The number of cards depends on your plan. Check **Billing** for your exact limit.
- Deleting a card frees up a slot.
- The Stackly watermark shows on Free and Starter. Upgrade to Growth or higher to remove it. <PlanBadge plan="Growth" />
- Photos must be under 5 MB.

## Troubleshooting
- **"You have reached your vCard limit"** → Delete a card you don't use or [upgrade your plan](./account/billing-and-plans).
- **The handle is taken** → Try a different **Subdomain Handle / Slug**.
- **Custom domain card doesn't open** → Add the wildcard DNS record shown in the form.
