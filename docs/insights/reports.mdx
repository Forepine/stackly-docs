The Reports page lists every click, one row each, with the time, city, country, device and browser. Filter it, then download it for your records or for a client.

In the left menu, under **Insights**, click **Reports**. You can also click **Reports** at the top of the [Analytics](./analytics) page.

<Shot id="insights/reports-page" title="Reports page with the date range buttons, summary cards and click table" />

## When to use it
- An agency needs a spreadsheet of last month's clicks for one client's link.
- A shop owner wants to check exactly when and from which city a QR code was scanned.

## Filter the clicks
1. Pick **Links** or **vCards** (visiting cards).
2. Pick a date range: **Today**, **Last 7 Days**, **Last 30 Days** or **This Month**. For other dates, click the date button, choose a start and end day, then click **Apply**.
3. To see one link only, type its back-half (the part after the slash) in **Filter by key**, for example `blog-link`. Click the **x** to clear it.

The cards at the top sum up your choice: **Clicks** (with unique visitors), **Links clicked**, **Top country** and **Top device**.

Each row shows **Resource / URL**, **Clicks**, **Device**, **Browser**, **City**, **Country** and **Timestamp**. On a wider screen you also see **Operating System**, **State / Region**, **Timezone**, **IP Address** and **User Agent** (the visitor's browser details). Switch between table and card view with the view buttons.

## Download a report
1. Click **Export**.
2. Pick the dates in the calendar. You can choose up to 3 months back.
3. Under **Export File Format**, pick **CSV** (opens in Excel or Google Sheets) or **JSON**.
   <Shot id="insights/export-modal" title="Export Links Report window with the date range and CSV selected" />
4. Click **Download Report**.

The file downloads to your device. The **Links** or **vCards** choice on the page decides which report you get.

## Reports for each client
There is no separate client folder on this page. To keep clients apart:
- Use **Filter by key** to show one client's link, then **Export**.
- Give a client a live page instead of a file: use **Share click report** on a link, or **Share report** on a campaign. See [Share a report with a client](./client-report-link).

## Good to know
- On the Free plan you see only the latest 10 clicks. Upgrade to see every click and export.
- Reports are not emailed or scheduled. Download them when you need them.
- Older clicks may have moved to your archive, depending on your plan. See [Data retention](../account/data-retention).

## Troubleshooting
- **The file is empty** → There were no clicks in those dates. Try a wider range or switch between **Links** and **vCards**.
- **I can't pick an older date** → The export goes back 3 months. For older clicks, check your [archive](../account/data-retention).
