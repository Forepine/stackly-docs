The Analytics page shows clicks and visitors across everything you share: links and visiting cards (vCards). In the left menu, under **Insights**, click **Analytics**.

<Shot id="insights/analytics-overview" title="Analytics page with the summary cards and the Overview tab" />

## The summary cards
- **Total clicks**: every click, with how many happened today.
- **Unique visitors**: different people, counted by their internet address (IP). One person clicking three times is three clicks but one visitor.
- **Last 7 days**: clicks this week, and the change against the 7 days before.
- **Countries**: how many countries your clicks came from.
- **Items clicked**: how many of your links and cards got clicks, plus how many UTM sources.

If you are the workspace owner, the filter at the top switches between **All Items** (the whole team) and **My Items** (only yours). Click the refresh icon to reload.

## The tabs
| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | **Clicks Over Time** day by day, **Current** (this month) or **Previous** (last month). **Top Links** and **Top VLinks** (top visiting cards). **Performance Analysis** by day of the week (**Weekly**, **This week** or **Last week**) or by month (**Monthly**, pick a year). |
| **Campaigns** | **UTM Campaign Analytics**: clicks by UTM source, medium and campaign. **Active Campaigns Performance**: clicks for each [campaign](../grow/campaigns). |
| **Geography** | **Global Traffic Map**. Click a country to see its states, then **Back to World View**. Also **State Distribution** and **City Distribution**. |
| **Devices** | Mobile, desktop and tablet (**Device Distribution**), **Operating Systems** and **Browser Distribution**. |
| **Advanced** | **Browsers & apps**: normal browsers, in-app visits (Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook and more) and bots. **Timezone Analytics**: which time zones your visitors are in. |

Most charts have a **Links** / **vCards** switch, a pie or bar view, and an expand button.

## Example: read your week
Riya runs a saree shop in Pune and shares one sale link on WhatsApp and Instagram.
1. **Last 7 days** shows 840 clicks, ▲ 30% vs the week before. The sale is working.
2. **Unique visitors** is 610, so some people came back more than once.
3. On **Advanced**, **Browsers & apps** shows most visits came from the WhatsApp and Instagram apps.
4. On **Geography**, **City Distribution** shows Pune first and Mumbai second, so she plans delivery to Mumbai.
5. On **Overview**, **Performance Analysis** shows Sunday is her best day, so she posts the next offer on Saturday night.

## Export your data
- To download every click as a file, go to [Reports](./reports) and click **Export** (CSV or JSON).
- To send links and clicks to Google Sheets, connect it in [Integrations](../workspace/integrations).

## How long data is kept
Each plan keeps your analytics for a set number of days. When your plan has a limit, a note at the top says "Showing the last … days". Every night, older clicks move to an archive, where they stay for 60 days and are then deleted. Click the note, or see [Data retention](../account/data-retention), to check your plan's window and download archived data.

## Good to know
- On the Free plan, the top lists show up to 10 items. You'll see **Upgrade to view complete analytics**.
- For one campaign's results, use the campaign's own **Analytics** page.
