---
title: Share a report with a client
description: Send a client a live, read-only click report for a link or campaign. They open it in any browser, with no Stackly account.
---

A report link is a read-only web page with the clicks for one link or one campaign. Anyone with the link can open it, with no login, and it stays up to date on its own.

## When to use it
- An agency sends a client a live report for their Diwali campaign instead of a new spreadsheet every week.
- A creator shows a brand how many people clicked the sponsored link.

## Create a report link
1. Open the **Share report** window:
   - For a link: on the **Links** page, open the link's menu and click **Share click report**.
   - For a campaign: open the campaign, click **Analytics**, then click **Share report**.
2. In **Title the client sees**, type a name, for example "Diwali campaign — weekly report".
3. Under **Link expires**, pick **Never**, **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**.
4. Optional: turn on **Hide Stackly branding** (Growth and above).
   <Shot id="insights/share-report-modal" title="Share report window with title, expiry and the Active links list" />
5. Click **Create report link**. The link is copied for you, ready to paste.

## Send, copy or turn off a link
Your links show under **Active links**, with the number of views and when each one expires.
- **Copy**: copy the link again.
- **WhatsApp**: send it in a WhatsApp chat.
- **Open**: see exactly what your client sees.
- **Turn off**: stop the link working. The client then sees "This report isn't available".

:::caution
Turning off a link can't be undone. To share again, create a new link.
:::

## What your client sees
<Shot id="insights/shared-report-page" title="Shared report page as a client sees it in the browser" />

- The title you chose and a date switch: **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** or **All time**.
- Cards for **Clicks**, **Avg per day**, and **Unique visitors** and **Top country** (for a link) or **Channels** and **Top channel** (for a campaign).
- **Clicks Over Time**, **Geographic Distribution**, **Top Referrers**, **Top cities**, **Devices**, **Browsers** and **Operating systems**.
- For a campaign, the **Channel Performance Comparison** too.

They can't edit anything or see your other links. They don't see visitors' IP addresses.

## Good to know
| Option | Plan |
|---|---|
| Report link for a link | All plans |
| Report link for a campaign | Growth and above (campaigns need Growth) |
| **Hide Stackly branding** | Growth and above |

- Report links have no password. Anyone who has the link can open it, so share it only with the right person.
- You can make more than one link for the same report, for example one per person, and turn each off on its own.

## Troubleshooting
- **Client sees "This report link is incomplete"** → The link was cut off when pasted. Click **Copy** and send it again.
- **Client sees "This report isn't available"** → The link expired or was turned off. Create a new one.
